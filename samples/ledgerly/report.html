Findings

  • A
    Ledgerly trails Cashvane in AI answers. Ledgerly appears in 40.3% of AI answers, ranging from 39.2% to 42.5% across runs. Cashvane appears in 61.8%, which shows clear room to grow.
  • B
    Comparison questions are the fastest area to win. In comparison questions, Ledgerly appears in 10.4% of answers, while Cashvane appears in 77.1%.
  • C
    Third-party sources shape the answers. AI answers cite softwarecompare.example in 50.4% of answers. Ledgerly's own site is cited in 9.3% of answers.

Quick wins

  • A
    Build a comparison hub. Publish honest comparison pages that place Ledgerly next to Cashvane and other options, with clear criteria and short summaries AI answers can quote. Supports finding A.
  • B
    Answer the gap questions directly. Create a page that answers "Are cloud treasury tools better than bank portals?" in its first paragraph, followed by a plain comparison table. Supports finding B.
  • C
    Earn coverage on softwarecompare.example. Pitch softwarecompare.example with product updates and customer data, so the sources AI engines already trust include Ledgerly. Supports finding C.

Methodology

Engines tested: OpenAI (gpt-6-luna, web search on); Anthropic Claude (claude-haiku-5-5, web search on); Google Gemini (gemini-3.8-flash, web search on); Perplexity (perplexity/sonar, web search on).

Run: November 17, 2026. 20 unbranded buyer questions, 3 runs per question on each engine, each run an independent call. 238 successful calls; 2 failed calls excluded from all metrics.

Brand names that are also common words required supporting context to count. Every number in this report comes from the audit's metrics file.

Limitations

  • API results can differ from consumer apps, which may personalize answers or use different models and search settings.
  • Google AI Overviews is not covered.
  • Results are a point-in-time snapshot and vary between runs.
  • This sample uses fictional companies and pre-written answers (mock mode). It does not describe any real company.
